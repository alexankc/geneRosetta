# geneRosetta: Gene Annotation Retrieval and Mapping Utilities

Reliable Gene Annotation Mapping. Retrieve, cache, and merge human,
mouse, and rat gene annotations from Ensembl BioMart, NCBI, and
organism-specific authorities (HGNC, MGI, RGD); map between gene symbols
and stable identifiers with synonym-aware matching and configurable
multi-mapping resolution.
